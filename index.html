Common Questions.

Does DeckPilot require Wi-Fi? expand_more

For local control, both devices should be on the same network. However, with the Desktop Pro relay, you can control your stream from anywhere in the world over the internet.

Which OBS versions are supported? expand_more

DeckPilot is fully compatible with OBS Studio 28.0 and higher, leveraging the native OBS WebSocket v5 integration.

Is there a limit to buttons or pages? expand_more

No. You can create unlimited decks and pages. Our "Smart Folders" help you organize complex setups easily.
